- **Samyong "✅ compatible" (survey) does not survive per-set arithmetic:** $0.25–0.50/pc × 30–40 pads = $7.50–20.00 FOB → $9.85–19.85 landed. Compatible only at the cheapest spec corner as a 500-pc pilot; not a production source at this pad count as listed.

- **Good Seller price discrepancy between the two inputs** ($0.93 anchor vs $1.69 @1k tier) — likely different listings/SKUs; resolved conservatively in favor of $1.69 and queued for RFQ confirmation.
