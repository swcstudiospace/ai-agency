# Supplier Vetting

## Checklist
1. Identity: store age, response quality, sample policy
2. Lead time P50/P90 and processing time
3. MOQ and sample cost
4. Quality: materials, packaging, branding (inserts/cards)
5. Shipping lanes + tracking quality by destination geo
6. Returns / defective process
7. Payment terms and scam signals

## Scoring
Use `score_supplier` tool. Prefer score ≥ 65 with a backup supplier.

## Test order protocol
1. Order 1–3 units to primary geo
2. Measure: transit days, packaging, product vs listing, insert quality
3. Photo evidence before scale PO
4. Never large prepay on untested supplier without human approval
