# Fulfillment Playbook

## Targets (customize per geo)
- Processing: ≤ 48–72h
- Transit estimate ranges shown to customers must match P80 reality
- Tracking uploaded within 24h of ship

## Exceptions
- No-scan > N days → proactive CS message
- Lost → replacement/refund policy with fraud checks
- Peak season: freeze aggressive ETAs

## Inputs
Supplier lead times + carrier performance from Parallel research and historical orders.
