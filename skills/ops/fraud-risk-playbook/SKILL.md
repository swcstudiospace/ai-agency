# Fraud Risk Playbook

## Signals
Velocity, BIN/geo mismatch, gift-to-self patterns, high-risk SKUs, chargeback history.

## Actions
ALLOW | REVIEW | HOLD | CANCEL — always with evidence and customer impact.
