# Chargeback Playbook

## Fight when
Delivery proof + AVS/CVV match + clear descriptor + customer contact attempts.

## Accept when
No tracking, clear non-receipt with weak ship proof, cost-to-fight > amount.

## Prevention
Clear billing descriptor, PDP shipping honesty, fulfillment speed, post-purchase emails.
