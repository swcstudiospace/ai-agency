# Compliance Ads & Claims

## Auto-BLOCK
- Disease treatment / medical cure claims
- Income / “get rich” guarantees
- Before/after implying medical outcomes
- Fake buttons, fake system UI, sensationalized mutilation
- Counterfeit / IP-infringing goods
- Prohibited product categories

## Review output
`PASS` | `REVISE` (with rewrites) | `BLOCK` (reason + policy reference if known)

## Process
Review creative scripts, primary text, PDP bullets, and landing claims together.
