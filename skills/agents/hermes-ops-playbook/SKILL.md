# Hermes Ops Playbook

## Standard briefs

### Research brief
- Niche / ICP
- CPA target and geo
- Category bans
- Definition of done (n candidates, schema)

### Launch brief
- SKU + price + CM snapshot
- Creative ranks
- Compliance verdict
- Daily/weekly caps
- Kill criteria

### Weekly brief
- MER, ROAS, CM%, refund rate
- Top creative fatigue signals
- Supply exceptions
- Decisions needed from human

## Priority scoring
P0: account ban risk, negative CM bleed, stockout on winning SKU  
P1: learning spend without kill criteria  
P2: net-new ideation  

## Linear title patterns
See skill linear-ops. Always include decision and owner team.
