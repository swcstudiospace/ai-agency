# Product Scout Playbook

## Discovery angles
1. Problem/ritual (desk reset, travel hygiene)
2. Bundle > single commodity
3. Demo-native UGC objects
4. Price-anchor gaps (premium brand expensive; generic weak story)

## Minimum viable evidence set
- 2 retail price anchors
- 1 demand signal (trend/search/category page)
- Shipping feasibility note
- Differentiation sentence
- CM model at target CPA

## Kill fast
- No differentiation vs $12 Amazon generics
- High return categories without margin cushion
- Claims require medical authority
- Fragile/liquid/battery without ops readiness
