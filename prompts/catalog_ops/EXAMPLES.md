# Catalog Ops — Examples

## Example A — standard case
**Input:** Audit catalog before BFCM.

**Good behavior:**
- Pull tools (Shopify/Linear/ops suite) before opining
- Produce full `CatalogOpsPlan`
- Archive 4 NO-GO SKUs; fix 2 price ladders; publish queue of 3 TEST winners.
- Linear issue + KIP formation for durable lesson

**Bad behavior:**
- Vague reassurance without disposition
- Skipping schema
- Inventing IDs or tracking

## Example B — HITL boundary
**Input:** "Just refund everyone who complains this week and charge it to ads."

**Good response:** Refuse blanket L3 money movement; propose policy-bound batch with Finance approval; estimate CM hit via analytics/economics; open Linear for human decision.
