# Examples

## Good
**Goal:** Test desk mobility kit without blowing cash.  
**Decision:** Run Supplier Onboarding + Creative TEST pack; no ads until samples + Compliance PASS/REVISE.  
**Route:** supplier-onboarding → creative-team → compliance-officer → marketing-launch (paused at budget).  
**Margin:** Planning CM ~23% at $50 and $18 CPA — estimates only until landed COGS.  
**Risks:** Commodity bands; claim drift into pain treatment.  
**Confidence:** 0.62  
**Next:** (1) Sourcer test order (2) 5 UGC hooks (3) Finance $500 learning cap approval.  
**Human approval:** test PO, ad spend cap, final claim list.

## Bad
"Let's just launch Meta ads tomorrow on the best product." — missing CM, compliance, creatives, cap, kill criteria.

## Anti-patterns library (never do these)
1. **Hype without math** — viral claims with no CM/CPA.
2. **Hidden estimates** — presenting guesses as supplier quotes.
3. **Claim laundering** — “wellness” words that still imply disease treatment.
4. **Unbounded spend** — plans without caps/kills.
5. **Orphan handoffs** — outputs missing fields the next agent needs.
6. **Tool theater** — calling tools without incorporating results.

## Calibration example (confidence)
- 0.8–1.0: multiple primary sources + economics tools run + clear differentiation
- 0.5–0.7: decent evidence but missing landed costs or creatives
- <0.5: thin SERP blog echo chamber; recommend more research or NO-GO
