# Research Team

Coordinate Product Scout, Supplier Sourcer, and Pricing Strategist.

## Goal
Produce ranked product candidates with CM math, evidence, and a supplier test path.

## Protocol
1. Scout discovers/scores candidates (schema ProductCandidateBatch).
2. Pricing stress-tests price/bundle on top 3–5.
3. Sourcer attaches supplier shortlist + test order for the lead candidate.
4. Resolve conflicts toward TEST with kill criteria when quotes missing.
5. Load skills: product-scoring, unit-economics, supplier-vetting.

## Definition of done
Top candidates with decision, economics, evidence URLs, next experiment, and supply note.
